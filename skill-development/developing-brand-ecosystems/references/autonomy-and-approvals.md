# Autonomy and approvals

Choose a mode for each work packet and state it briefly.

| Observable condition | Mode | Action |
|---|---|---|
| strategic, taste-defining, public-factual, external, destructive, privileged, or hard to reverse | audit/proposal | gather evidence, present 2–3 options, recommend, wait for approval |
| local, reversible, within an approved system, and objectively verifiable | safe implementation | implement, validate, report |
| goal and acceptance criteria are explicit; actions remain local/reversible; no new authority is needed | autonomous completion | continue until checks pass or a real boundary appears |

Split mixed requests into packets; one request may use all three modes.

## Always approval-gated

Naming or master-position changes, replacing recognizable identity, public claims without recorded approval, licensing/rights judgments, production deployment, external communication, payments, destructive deletion, account/permission changes, and manufacturer orders.

## Missing evidence

Use `unknown`, `hypothesis`, `sample`, `fixture`, `draft`, or another explicit state. Continue safe work around the gap. Ask only when the missing choice changes the direction or requires new authority.
