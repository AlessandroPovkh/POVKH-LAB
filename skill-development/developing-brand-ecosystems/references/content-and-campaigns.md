# Content and campaigns

Status: `original-method`

## Skill attribution

**Primary owner:** `developing-brand-ecosystems` owns the canonical-fact, fixture, consumer, approval, and cross-channel consistency method. A specialist writing or data skill owns execution only when explicitly invoked; none is evidenced as historical creator of POVKH LAB campaign files.

**Evidence:** `campaigns/PVKH-001/`, `content-system/`, `media/campaign-assets/PVKH-001/`, `site/data/catalog.json`, and the ecosystem audits prove the content systems and fact/fixture boundary.

**Added by `developing-brand-ecosystems`:** A reusable structured relationship between approved facts, planning data, channel outputs, owners, schedules, metrics, and delivery validation.

## Content system

Define message hierarchy, voice, vocabulary, editorial formats, channel roles, cadence, naming, approvals, localization, accessibility, and evidence rules. Keep approved facts in structured sources when several outputs reuse them.

## Campaign packet

Create only relevant components:

- brief and objective;
- audience and message;
- canonical facts and approval status;
- asset matrix and owners;
- channel adaptations;
- schedule and dependencies;
- copy deck;
- measurement definitions;
- delivery checklist and archive rules.

Distinguish observed metrics, targets, forecasts, samples, and placeholders. Never present a projection or demonstration partner as an achieved fact.

## Validation

Cross-check every reusable fact across product, site, campaign, media kit, metadata, structured data, and exports. Validate links, dates, names, legal lines, translations, asset dimensions, and platform-safe areas.
