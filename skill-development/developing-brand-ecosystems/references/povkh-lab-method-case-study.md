## Chronology

1. Establish a laboratory/editorial character, positioning, naming, and catalog logic.
2. Explore ten visual directions on comparable artifacts; approve one Typographic Monolith master and archive alternatives.
3. Codify logo hierarchy, color roles, typography, grid, graphic language, cover system, voice, and production rules in one normative guide.
4. Keep editable SVG sources separate from outlined production masters, raster exports, previews, and concept archives. Distribute local fonts with SIL OFL licenses.
5. Extend the system into release campaigns, motion, a multilingual static site, EPK, sonic identity, onboarding, content operations, photo/video, physical/merch, and an operations dashboard.
6. Store approved site catalog facts separately from a demonstration campaign's planning fixture. Mark unknowns and gates instead of inventing public data.
7. Generate site pages, waveforms, streaming media, visuals, PDFs, motion, and dashboards through reproducible scripts and manifests.
8. Verify accessibility, responsive layout, routes, links, media metadata, file naming, geometry, source fingerprints, and cross-system boundaries.
9. Keep the site in preview/noindex mode until domain, contact, public links, and production approval are resolved.
10. Provide an ecosystem index, commands, audits, masters, licenses, and a vibe-coder handoff.

## Transferable principles

- One authority per rule or fact.
- Several directions before one master.
- Real content reveals system defects.
- Source, master, export, preview, fixture, and archive are different states.
- Accessibility and reproducibility are brand quality.
- Launch gates prevent templates from masquerading as production.
- Integration QA catches contradictions that artifact-by-artifact review misses.
