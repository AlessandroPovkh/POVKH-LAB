# Baseline observations

Three fresh agents answered without `developing-brand-ecosystems`.

## Fragmented B2B SaaS

The response immediately invented a market position, slogan, palette, typeface, five-day schedule, and redesign direction from a one-line brief. It labeled the position a hypothesis but still prescribed production choices before resolving existing evidence, brand equity, repository state, or approval authority.

## Existing consumer identity

The response preserved recognizable codes and separated legal/business changes, but assumed commerce journeys not present in the prompt and lacked an explicit source/master/export map. Validation was strong at the digital artifact level but did not require downstream ecosystem reconciliation.

## Cultural project with mixed facts

The response correctly separated confirmed facts, drafts, KPIs, and demonstration partners. It proposed approval roles and a minimally truthful launch, but treated the fix as a one-time checklist instead of a durable structured source, generator, validator, and recurring governance loop.

## Required corrections

The skill must require evidence before invention, preserve existing character by default, choose autonomy per packet, apply YAGNI to touchpoints, resolve source/master/export states, and require both local and integration validation.
